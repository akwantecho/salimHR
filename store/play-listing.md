### Full description — max 4000
```
Salim HR is a complete administration app for physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinics, connecting management, reception, and specialists in one place.

▪️ For Specialists
• Daily session schedule with treatment-room selection
• One-tap confirmation of today's sessions
• Incoming transfer requests (accept/reject with a reason)
• Requests: leave, salary advance, patient transfer, and notes to management

▪️ For Reception
• Book appointments and view the specialists' timetable
• Mark patient attendance / absence
• Inventory requests
• Full patient file with session history

▪️ For Management
• Dashboard with clinic statistics
• Approve or reject all requests (leave, advances, inventory, transfers)
• Reply to employee notes
• Record expenses

▪️ For everyone
• Instant push notifications with sound for every decision or update
• Full Arabic/English interface
• Fast, modern design

Salim HR is an internal work tool used with an account provided by your workplace.
```
